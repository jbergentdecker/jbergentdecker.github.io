About this Tour

The Großglockner (3,798 m) is the highest peak in Austria and one of the most iconic summits in the Eastern Alps. This route follows the classic Normalweg from the Lucknerhaus, passing through the Lucknerhütte and the Stüdlhütte before tackling the glacier section to the summit.

Route Description

Starting from the Lucknerhaus (1,918 m), the trail climbs steadily through alpine meadows toward the Lucknerhütte (2,241 m). From there, the terrain becomes rockier as you gain altitude toward the Stüdlhütte (3,060 m), a good place to rest before the glacier.

The glacier crossing requires crampons and an ice axe. Fixed ropes assist on the steepest sections near the Glocknerleitl. The final ridge to the summit is exposed but well-marked. The views from the top stretch across the Hohe Tauern in all directions.

Practical Info

  • Start / End: Lucknerhaus, Kals am Großglockner
  • Best season: July – September
  • Gear: Crampons, ice axe, helmet, harness
  • Overnight: Stüdlhütte (recommended for early summit start)
  • Permit: None required
